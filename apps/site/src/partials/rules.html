Rules it can't break.

An assistant that answers customers has to be more careful than a chatbot. These are built into the software, not into a prompt.

Draft-only until you say otherwise

You choose one of three modes, and every new account starts in approve-everything, so nothing is sent without your click.

No invented prices, dates or promises

Every number in a reply must trace back to a line in your knowledge base. If it can't, the reply is held and you're asked.

Sensitive e-mails go to a human

Complaints, refunds, legal questions, discount requests and angry messages are never answered automatically.

Your data stays yours

Your data is stored in the EU (Frankfurt). E-mail text is deleted after 90 days by default. One button deletes everything, for good.
