One assistant, every repetitive job.

Most lost sales are e-mails that were answered too late or never followed up. Noctiv does the boring part of running an inbox, and the paperwork that follows, every day, at any hour.

Replies within minutes

Every inquiry gets a reply in the customer's language, built only from your website, price list and notes. If the answer isn't there, Noctiv asks you instead of guessing.

Follow-ups on autopilot

No reply after three days? Noctiv sends a short, personal nudge during business hours, up to twice. You decide when it stops.

A lead list that fills itself

Every conversation becomes a lead with a stage: received, replied, followed up, won. No CRM to set up, no data entry.

Quotes, invoices and documents

Price requests become quotes from your own price list, with an Accept button. Accepted quotes become invoices, paid ones get their delivery note: numbered PDFs with your logo.

Talks to you

Ask the Noctiv Assistant how last week went, or tell it what to do in your own words. It prepares the invoice, the e-mail or the setting; nothing happens until you confirm.
