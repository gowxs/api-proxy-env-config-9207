Connect Zoho Mail.

Zoho Mail connects with IMAP and an App Password. Turn IMAP on, generate the password, and paste it into Noctiv.

1. Turn on IMAP access

In Zoho Mail, open Settings → Mail Accounts, choose your address and make sure IMAP Access is switched on.

2. Generate an App Password

  1. Open accounts.zoho.com (your Zoho account) and go to Security.
  2. Open App Passwords and choose Generate New Password.
  3. Name it "Noctiv" and copy the password Zoho shows.

3. Connect in Noctiv

In Settings → Mailboxes, choose Other (IMAP/SMTP), enter your address and the App Password, and the servers below. If you give the Noctiv Assistant your address, it recognises Zoho from your domain and fills them in.

  • Incoming (IMAP): imap.zoho.com, port 993 (EU accounts: imap.zoho.eu)
  • Outgoing (SMTP): smtp.zoho.com, port 465 (EU accounts: smtp.zoho.eu)

Business accounts on your own domain may be given different server names by Zoho. If the connection test fails, check the servers shown in Zoho Mail → Settings → Mail Accounts → IMAP and use those.

Noctiv tests reading and sending before it saves anything, and stores the password encrypted. To disconnect, delete the App Password in your Zoho account.
