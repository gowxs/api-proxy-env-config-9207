Your data and GDPR.

Database in the EU, kept only as long as needed, never used to train AI, and deleted with one button. The full details are in the privacy notice.

Who is responsible

For the e-mails in the mailbox you connect, you are the controller and Noctiv processes them on your behalf, under our data processing agreement. For your account and this website, we are the controller.

Noctiv also reads your Sent folder, read-only, so that replies you send from your own mail client appear in the conversation. It keeps only replies that belong to a conversation Noctiv already has, and discards all other sent mail without storing it. It mirrors whether an incoming e-mail is marked as read, and never changes anything in your mailbox.

Where it is

Your database is in the EU, in Frankfurt, Germany. E-mails are read and replies drafted by Google Cloud (Vertex AI) in the EU. While we finish our infrastructure, application servers run temporarily in the United Kingdom, which has an EU adequacy decision. The privacy notice lists every provider.

How long

  • E-mail text: 90 days by default; you can shorten it in Settings.
  • Your account and knowledge base: until you delete them.
  • Invoices and other documents are business records and are kept until you delete them.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, show ads, or use your e-mails to train AI models. Notification e-mails show only the sender's domain and a summary, unless you switch on full text.

Shopify order data

If you connect Shopify, order data is fetched live, read-only, when a customer asks about an order, and only if the sender's e-mail matches the order. We do not copy orders into our database. We store the encrypted access token per store, a short per-message summary (deleted with the message under your retention period) and an access-log entry without personal data. Disconnecting, uninstalling the app or a Shopify erasure request deletes the token.

WooCommerce order data

If you connect WooCommerce, order data is fetched live and read-only from your store when a customer asks about an order, and only if the sender's e-mail matches the billing e-mail on the order. We do not copy orders into our database. We store your REST key encrypted per store, a short per-message summary (deleted with the message under your retention period) and an access-log entry without personal data. Disconnecting deletes the key; delete it in WooCommerce as well, because only you can revoke it there.

Delete everything

Settings → Account has one button that deletes your business's data, for good. You can also revoke Noctiv's access at any time by deleting its App Password at your e-mail provider. To see, correct or export your data, write to contact@noctiv.io; we answer within one month.
