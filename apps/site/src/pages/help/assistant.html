The Noctiv Assistant.

Talk to Noctiv in your own words, in your own language. It answers questions about your business and prepares what you ask for as a card; nothing happens until you confirm.

What you can ask

  • "How did last week go?" or "Which invoices are unpaid?"
  • "Send Anna an invoice for €290 for website development, due in 7 days."
  • "Mark invoice INV-2026-0012 as paid."
  • "Add a note: we're closed from 24 to 26 December."
  • "Switch off the Monday summary."
  • "My e-mail is hello@yourshop.com": it opens the mailbox form, filled in.

Cards you confirm

The assistant proposes; you decide. Each action appears as a card you can check, and it only happens when you press Confirm: changing a setting, adding a knowledge note or price-list item, creating an invoice or delivery note (ready, with its number and PDF), writing an e-mail to a customer (sent from your mailbox only after you press Send), or marking an invoice as paid.

What it can't do

It can't approve or reject reply drafts, cancel or edit documents, or change your billing. Those stay in the app, where you can see everything.

Where to find it

Open "Noctiv Assistant" in the app, on your phone or computer. New accounts can also set up Noctiv by chatting with it instead of filling in the setup steps.
