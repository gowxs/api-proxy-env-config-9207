Connect Shopify for order lookups.

Noctiv can answer "Where is my order?" from your live Shopify data. It only reads. It never changes anything in your store.

Connect (on your phone is fine)

  1. Open Integrations in Noctiv and tap Connect Shopify.
  2. Shopify asks you to approve one permission: read orders. Tap Install.
  3. You come back to Noctiv. Sign in and confirm which business the store belongs to.
  4. Tap Test connection. It should say it can read your orders.

What Noctiv reads

Order number, date, payment and delivery status, carrier, tracking number and link, expected delivery and the names of the shipped items. Never payment details or full addresses.

When it answers and when it hands over to you

Noctiv answers only when the sender's e-mail matches the e-mail on the order. If it does not match, nothing about the order is revealed and the customer is asked for the order number and checkout e-mail. It hands over to you when the order is not found, cancelled, refunded, partly shipped, shipped without tracking, shipped more than the number of days you set in Settings (default 14), when the customer asks for a refund, return or address change, or sounds angry or mentions a chargeback. It never guesses.

Disconnect

In Integrations tap Disconnect and delete token. We revoke the access at Shopify and delete it. Uninstalling the app in Shopify does the same.
