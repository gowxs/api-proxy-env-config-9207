Noctiv · design system v1

Night, then morning.

The site tells one story: e-mail arrives at night, a reply is ready by morning. Everything here serves that story and gets out of its way.

  1. Night → morning. Deep indigo for the hours Noctiv works; warm dawn light only where the work is done.
  2. One typeface. Manrope, self-hosted, variable weight. Tabular figures for every time and price.
  3. Rules, not cards. Content sits on hairlines. A surface appears only when it is a real object: a message, the price, a form.
  4. One moving thing. The overnight inbox in the hero. Nothing else animates.
  5. Calm contrast. Every text colour passes WCAG AA; body text passes AAA.

07 · The one moving thing

The overnight inbox

A 14-second loop: the e-mail arrives at 23:41, Noctiv drafts at 23:42, the clock runs to 08:05 as the sky turns to morning, and the reply is approved. It pauses off-screen, has a Pause button, and shows the finished morning state when reduced motion is on.
