Rules, not guesses.

Only the owner of the order

The order is used only if the sender's e-mail matches the order. Otherwise nothing is revealed and the customer is asked for the order number and checkout e-mail.

Only facts

Status, carrier, tracking and expected delivery from the store. No payment details, no addresses. The wording is checked against those facts.

You get the hard cases

Not found, cancelled, refunded, partly shipped, no tracking, a late parcel, refund or return requests and angry e-mails are handed to you.
