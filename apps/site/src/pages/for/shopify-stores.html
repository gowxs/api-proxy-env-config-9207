The e-mails a Shopify store gets every day.

Most of them are the same few questions, asked at 23:00 by someone deciding whether to check out. Noctiv answers from what your store already says, in the shopper's language.

Shipping times and costs

"Do you ship to Austria? How long does it take?" Answered from your shipping page: zones, prices, dispatch times and carriers, exactly as you wrote them.

Product questions before buying

Sizes, materials, care, what's in the box, whether two items fit together. If your product pages or notes don't say it, Noctiv asks you instead of guessing.

Returns and exchanges

How many days, who pays the return label, how to start one. The policy question is answered; an actual refund request always comes to you.

Payment and discount codes

Which payment methods you accept, whether a code can be combined. Requests for a discount are never answered automatically: they are yours to decide.

Wholesale and bulk orders

A café wants 40 mugs? With Quotes (beta) switched on, Noctiv prepares a quote from your price list as a PDF with an Accept link, and waits for your approval.

"Where is my order?"

Noctiv can't look up Shopify orders yet (the integration is marked coming soon). General delivery times are answered from your policy; a question about one specific order is passed to you with a short summary.

An example reply.

A shopper writes late on a Sunday. The draft is ready a minute later, built only from the store's shipping and returns pages, and waits for the owner's click in the morning.

Example store and numbers. Noctiv uses your own, and checks every number in a draft against your knowledge base.

No Shopify app to install.

Noctiv works on the mailbox, not inside Shopify. You connect the address customers write to (the one on your contact page and in your order e-mails) with an App Password, and add your store's URL: Noctiv reads the shipping, returns, FAQ and product pages and keeps them fresh. Add notes for anything the site doesn't say.

Shoppers who asked a question and didn't reply get a short, personal follow-up after three days, sent during business hours, at most twice. It stops the moment they answer. Every conversation lands in a simple lead list with its stage: received, replied, followed up, won.
