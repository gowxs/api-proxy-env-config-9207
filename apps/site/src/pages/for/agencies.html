From inquiry to paid, in one place.

Every inquiry becomes a lead with a stage: received, replied, followed up, quoted, accepted, won. When the client accepts a quote online, they enter their billing details on the Accept page, and Noctiv can issue the invoice with your logo, number it, and send it for your approval. Payments that arrive by bank transfer are recognised from your bank's notification e-mails and matched to the invoice.

You can also just tell the Noctiv Assistant what to do: "Send Marta an invoice for the deposit, €950, due in 7 days." It prepares the invoice and the e-mail as cards; nothing is created or sent until you confirm. Every Monday, a short summary shows what Noctiv did the week before: e-mails answered, follow-ups sent, quotes accepted and invoices paid.
