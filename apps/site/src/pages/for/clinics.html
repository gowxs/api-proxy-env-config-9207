Treatment prices

"How much is a first consultation?" Answered from your published prices, with what's included. A price that isn't in your knowledge base is never guessed.

Opening hours and location

Hours, holidays, parking, which entrance, whether there's step-free access. Written once in your notes, answered every time.

How to book

Noctiv doesn't book appointments or see your calendar. It sends people to your online booking page or phone number, exactly as your notes say.

Preparing for a visit

What to bring, how early to arrive, how long a session takes, your cancellation policy. The practical questions your reception answers by heart.

Payment and insurance

Which payment methods you accept, and what your notes say about insurers or receipts. Anything about a specific claim goes to you.

Anything about health

Symptoms, results, medication, "is this normal?": for these, keep mode 1 so every reply waits for a person. Urgent messages always come straight to you.

Patient e-mails need care with data.

E-mails to a clinic can contain health information. Noctiv's database is in the EU (Frankfurt, Germany), keeps e-mail text for 90 days by default (you can shorten this), never uses your e-mails to train AI, and deletes everything with one button. Notifications show only the sender's domain and a summary unless you switch on full text. You remain the controller for your patients' data and Noctiv processes it on your behalf under a data processing agreement; read data and GDPR and check it against your own rules before you connect a mailbox that receives medical records.

Many practices connect a general enquiries address (info@ or hello@) rather than a clinician's own mailbox. That keeps the routine questions with Noctiv and the clinical correspondence where it already is.
