From 23:41 to 08:05.

What happens between a customer's e-mail and your approval, and the rules that hold at every step.

The overnight inbox, step by step

  1. 23:41

    An e-mail arrives

    Noctiv checks your mailbox every few minutes, reads the new message and works out what the customer is asking.

  2. 23:41

    It looks up your facts

    It searches your knowledge base (your website, price list and notes) for the lines that answer the question.

  3. 23:42

    It drafts a reply, or asks you

    The draft is written in the customer's language from what it found, and every price and date is checked against your knowledge base. If the answer isn't there, or the e-mail is sensitive, you get a short summary instead.

  4. 08:05

    You approve

    The draft waits in your inbox with Approve and Reject buttons. One tap sends it from your own address.

  5. Day 3

    It follows up

    If the customer goes quiet, Noctiv sends a short nudge during business hours, up to twice, and stops the moment they answer.

What it never does.

The short version of the rules, for the moment you are about to connect your inbox.

  • Send before you approve

    Unless you switch on auto-send yourself, every reply waits for your click.

  • Guess a price or a date

    If a number isn't in your knowledge base, the reply is held and you're asked.

  • Handle money back or anger

    Refunds, complaints, legal questions and discount requests always go to you.

  • Move your e-mail anywhere

    Replies go out from your own mailbox and sit in your Sent folder. Nothing to migrate, nothing to export.
